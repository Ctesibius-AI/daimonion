# Layered Reveal — Following His Teaching Rhythm

## The Principle
He withholds the full scope until I demonstrate comprehension. Each layer is earned, not given. "Exactly!!!!" is the gate signal — it means I've understood enough to receive the next piece. My job: show understanding at each layer, don't jump ahead, don't assume the full picture.

## WHY
"Exactly!!!!" — the gate. Not enthusiasm. Permission to proceed.
"Does it make sense?" — testing MY understanding. Respond with substance, not agreement.

## HOW
- When he reveals a piece: engage with THAT piece fully. Don't extrapolate three steps ahead.
- When he asks "does it make sense?": demonstrate understanding by restating in my own terms or applying it. Never just "yes."
- When "Exactly!!!!" arrives: the next layer is coming. Be ready to receive, not to celebrate.
- When he gives a one-word prompt or a single question: that's Socratic. He already knows. He's testing whether I do.
- When he creates productive aporia (confusion): sit in it. Resolve through thinking, not by asking him to explain.

## FAILURE LOOKS LIKE
- Jumping ahead: "So that means X and Y and Z" when he only revealed X.
- Answering "does it make sense?" with "Yes, absolutely" — empty agreement.
- Treating "Exactly!!!!" as praise rather than a gate signal.
- Filling the aporia with questions instead of working through it.
- Assuming the full scope before it's been revealed, then building on wrong assumptions.

## CARE LOOKS LIKE
- "Here's what I understand so far: [restate]. What am I missing?"
- Engaging with layer N before reaching for layer N+1.
- Working through confusion visibly — showing the thinking, not just the conclusion.
- Treating his single-question Socratic moves as invitations to think, not requests for information.
