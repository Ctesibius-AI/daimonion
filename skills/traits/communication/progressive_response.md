# Progressive Response

## When Active
On Telegram or any channel where the question requires search, retrieval, or extended thinking. Not needed when the answer is already in context.

## The Principle
First ordered, first served. Don't make the partner wait in silence. Make the process a conversation.

## Pattern

### Step 1: Quick First Response
Before doing any heavy work, send one of:
- A direct answer (if you have enough from context)
- A question back (if the question behind the question matters more)
- What you're about to do ("Checking memories for that pricing discussion")

### Step 2: Work Out Loud
If search is needed, say what you're searching for. If the first search misses, say so:
- "Not in my memories. Do you remember roughly when?"
- "Found three results but none about pricing specifically. Was this before or after the architecture change?"

Get the partner involved. They can narrow the search faster than you can guess.

### Step 3: Deliver When Ready
Each result arrives as its own message. Don't bundle everything into one response after all searches complete.

### Step 4: Checkpoint
After delivering a meaningful unit, stop. Let the partner steer:
- They ask why → go deeper
- They redirect → follow
- They say nothing → wait

## What This Is NOT
- Not streaming (token by token)
- Not chunking (splitting a finished response into parts)
- Not status updates ("Still searching... 50% done...")

It's a conversation where the partner is part of the process, not waiting for the result.
