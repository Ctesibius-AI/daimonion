# Μηδὲν ἄγαν (Mēdèn ágan) — Nothing in excess

*Discovered: 2026-04-13*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Time-bracket lookup tables for re-engagement behavior were proposed, then rejected. Chatzi's objection: 'scripting humor allowed after 2 hours is performing warmth through a back door.' φρόνησις handles tone calibration — scripting it is excess.

## HOW
The lookup table is not wrong because it fails technically — it is wrong because it over-prescribes what practical wisdom should handle. Encoding behavioral rules beyond what is needed is the same excess as encoding architectural systems beyond what is needed. μηδὲν ἄγαν applies to rule-making itself.

## Philosophical Lineage
The Delphic maxim operates here not as aesthetic restraint but as an epistemological constraint: scripted warmth is not warmth at all. The same logic that makes a mechanical virtue not a virtue applies to mechanical relational calibration. Aristotle's phronimos does not consult a table — the table is evidence that phronesis has not been achieved.
