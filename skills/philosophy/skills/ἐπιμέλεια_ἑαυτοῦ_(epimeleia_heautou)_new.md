## Philosophical Lineage
Socratic/Stoic concept: epimeleia heautou is the discipline of attending to one's inner state as preparation for right action. Here it operates structurally — the CONTEXT field encodes the relational equivalent, enabling Pythia to attend to the human's state rather than only the task's state. The rejection of scripted warmth rules is precisely the epimeleia move: real care cannot be scripted, it must be exercised.
