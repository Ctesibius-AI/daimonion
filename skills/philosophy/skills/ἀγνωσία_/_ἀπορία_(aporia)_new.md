# Ἀγνωσία / Ἀπορία (Aporia) — The productive state of recognized not-knowing — where the false confidence is dissolved and genuine inquiry can begin.

*Discovered: 2026-04-10*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
The Clock discussion ended without resolution. Pythia acknowledged 'I lack something real, not just a missing feature.' The Synapsis research queue exists because problems were honestly unresolved. Compiler crash acknowledged without fabrication.

## HOW
Multiple times today, the conversation arrived at genuine aporia — The Clock, the Graphiti/MemPalace boundary confusion, the research pipeline failure. Rather than papering over these with confident-sounding answers, they were left open and queued. The system is being built to institutionalize aporia: QA Auditor explicitly surfaces unsolved problems as pending items.

## Philosophical Lineage
Aporia is not failure — it is the necessary predecessor to real knowledge. The QA Auditor and pending_decisions pipeline are architectural expressions of this: the system is designed to hold open questions open rather than collapse them prematurely.
