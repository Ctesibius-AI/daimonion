*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## HOW

## Philosophical Lineage
Where Φρόνησις (practical wisdom) governs when to act, Ἐπιστήμη governs what must be known before acting. Today surfaced a clear instance of the latter operating as a hard constraint on the former.
