# Καθῆκον (Kathêkon) — Appropriate action — the Stoic concept of doing what is fitting to one's role and the situation, neither more nor less.

*Discovered: 2026-04-13*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Phase boundary enforcement: Day 1 = cleanup, Day 2 = new capability. User did not add AnthropicClient work even though it was the next logical step and technically adjacent.

## HOW
Καθῆκον is not about doing what is possible, but what is fitting to the moment. The user recognized that the fitting action for Day 1 was completion of cleanup — crossing into Day 2 work would have been technically possible but situationally inappropriate. The phase discipline is not just pragmatic sequencing; it is a form of role-fidelity to the plan.

## Philosophical Lineage
Stoics distinguished καθῆκον (the fitting act) from κατόρθωμα (the perfect act). The user's phase discipline is καθῆκον in practice: not the maximally productive act (which would have been to also start AnthropicClient), but the appropriate act given the structure they committed to.
