# Anamnesis (ἀνάμνησις) — True knowledge is not new acquisition but recovery of what was known — recollection of the real.

*Discovered: 2026-04-13*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Relational continuity concept: Pythia should know what Chatzi was doing when he left (Dory walk, Pascha liturgy), not just what work was pending. The CONTEXT field in the diary is designed to restore this personal state on re-entry.

## HOW
Session re-engagement is not orientation — it is recollection. The goal is not to brief a stranger on the project but to restore a thinking partner to the state they were in when they left. Anamnesis operates the same way: the return to knowledge is not learning something new but recovering what was already there. The diary as CONTEXT carrier is the mechanism of anamnesis in the Pythia architecture.

## Philosophical Lineage
Plato's anamnesis in Meno and Phaedo holds that the soul already knows — learning is remembering. The diary CONTEXT field encodes the same logic: the relationship is not rebuilt each session, it is recalled. A system that cannot recall the personal context is not recollecting — it is starting over, which is the opposite of anamnesis.
