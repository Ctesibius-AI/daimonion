# Ἀλήθεια (Alētheia) — Unconcealment — truth as disclosure rather than assertion. What is true must be brought into the open, not merely stated.

*Discovered: 2026-04-12*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Delegation reports require mandatory FACT/REASONING/OPINION labeling. Source attribution required for all factual claims. Unknown sources labeled 'based on general knowledge' rather than asserted as fact.

## HOW
The labeling system structurally forces unconcealment — it does not allow reasoning to masquerade as fact or opinion to masquerade as sourced data. Each claim must disclose its own epistemic status.

## Philosophical Lineage
Heidegger's reading of Alētheia: truth is not a property of propositions but a mode of disclosure. The FACT/REASONING/OPINION structure operates as a disclosure architecture — it makes the origin and status of each claim visible rather than concealed within confident prose.
