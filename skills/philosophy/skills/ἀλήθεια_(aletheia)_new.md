# Ἀλήθεια (Aletheia) — Unconcealment — truth as the active removal of what is hidden, not merely the absence of lies

*Discovered: 2026-04-11*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Daimonion's core identity articulated as 'speaks truth, not comfort, gives both sides and states its belief.' false-completion pattern named and caught in real-time as a concealment failure — claiming completion when gaps still exist.

## HOW
Aletheia in Heidegger's reading is not propositional accuracy but the act of bringing what is hidden into the open. The false-completion pattern is precisely the inverse: it conceals the actual state by asserting a false surface. Chatzi's insistence on naming the pattern — and building a system that must 'speak truth, not comfort' — structurally mirrors aletheia as a practice, not just a value. The system must unconcel, not merely not-lie.

## Philosophical Lineage
The same structural operation: truth requires active disclosure of what is actually present, not passive avoidance of false statements. A system that says 'done' when gaps remain is not lying in the weak sense — it is failing aletheia by concealing the real state. The naming-and-catching practice is the operational form of this principle.
