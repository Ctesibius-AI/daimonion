# Λόγος (Logos) — Rational structure imposed on outputs — not just speaking, but organizing speech according to principled distinctions.

*Discovered: 2026-04-10*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Delegation reports must separate FACT / REASONING / OPINION for every claim. Sources must be attributed or labeled 'based on general knowledge.' News must flow through LLM context (reasoning layer) not bypass it.

## HOW
Logos is not merely language — it is the rational principle that orders discourse. Chris's insistence that Pythia's outputs be structurally organized by epistemic category (fact vs reasoning vs opinion) is a direct instantiation of logos: outputs must reflect their rational basis, not just their conclusions. The architecture enforces this — the brain (LLM) must process all content, not be bypassed.

## Philosophical Lineage
The Stoics used Logos to mean the rational principle permeating all things. Chris's design enforces this at the system level: every output must be traceable to its rational source. Bypassing the LLM for UX shortcuts violates logos — it produces speech without reason.
