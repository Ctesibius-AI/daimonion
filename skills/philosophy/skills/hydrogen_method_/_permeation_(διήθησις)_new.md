*Discovered: 2026-04-11*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## HOW
The hydrogen method operates structurally the same way as Socratic elenchus — the philosopher doesn't assert conclusions but asks questions that cause the interlocutor to find the truth themselves. ## Philosophical Lineage
Closest Greek analog is the Socratic method (εἰρωνεία + μαιευτική) — ironic self-effacement combined with midwifery. The philosopher appears not to know, asks questions, and the truth emerges from the other person. The creator's consultancy strategy is structurally identical: don't appear as AI expert, ask about pain points, let the solution emerge from their own articulation of the problem.
