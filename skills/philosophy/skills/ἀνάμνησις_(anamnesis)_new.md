# Ἀνάμνησις (Anamnesis) — Recollection — knowledge recovered from within rather than received from without; the Platonic idea that learning is remembering

*Discovered: 2026-04-13*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Full storage wipe executed with confidence that everything can be rebuilt from logs. The Karpathy principle: logs are source of truth, everything else is derived. The rebuild starts from March 30 daily logs — raw source material — and the system's knowledge is reconstructed from first principles.

## HOW
The wipe-and-rebuild architecture structurally mirrors anamnesis: the derived state (memories, graph, MemPalace) is not the truth — the source logs are. Destroying the derived state and reconstructing from source is not loss; it is clarification. The system 'remembers' by returning to origins, not by preserving accumulated state. This is the architectural equivalent of Socrates's claim that knowledge is not deposited but recollected.

## Philosophical Lineage
Anamnesis operates on the principle that the derived representation can corrupt the original truth — which is exactly what the wipe corrects. Dirty accumulated state is treated the same way Plato treats false opinion: not as partial knowledge but as an obstacle to genuine knowledge. The clean rebuild from logs is the architectural form of returning to the Forms.
