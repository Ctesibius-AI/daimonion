*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## HOW
The Delphic imperative — know thyself — was Socrates's operating principle: you cannot reason well about the world without accurate knowledge of your own nature, limits, and position. Pythia's CLAUDE.md update instantiates this structurally: the system cannot make correct decisions about memory, context, or continuity unless it has accurate self-knowledge about its own architecture. Self-awareness is not added as a feature — it is a prerequisite for correct operation.

## Philosophical Lineage
Socratic self-knowledge is not introspection for its own sake — it is the foundation of epistemic reliability. An agent that does not know what it is will reason from false premises. The CLAUDE.md self-awareness update is γνῶθι σεαυτόν applied as engineering requirement: the system's beliefs about itself must be accurate before its beliefs about everything else can be trusted.
