# μηδὲν ἄγαν — Nothing in excess — optimal function through constraint, not through maximization

*Discovered: 2026-04-10*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Single-option questions enforced mid-session, terse confirmations ('y', 'cont', 'y and continue'), one-question rule given as explicit behavioral feedback and immediately applied

## HOW
Chatzi's interaction design actively removes excess: excess options, excess words, excess questions. This is not minimalism as aesthetic — it is a functional principle that maximum information density per exchange requires removing everything that isn't load-bearing.

## Philosophical Lineage
μηδὲν ἄγαν (inscribed at Delphi alongside γνῶθι σεαυτόν) operates as a design constraint, not just a moral one. In Chatzi's system, excess in the interface creates noise that degrades signal — the same structural logic as the Delphic warning against hubris through overreach. The Oracle spoke in compressed form for epistemic reasons, not brevity for its own sake.
