# Pistis (πίστις) — Trust grounded in demonstrable evidence, not assertion

*Discovered: 2026-04-13*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
The circle not closing (Synapsis writes intelligence to JSON but not back to MemPalace) was named as a structural failure. AAAK index uses TBD vs N/A to distinguish 'not yet processed' from 'processed and excluded' — the distinction preserves evidential integrity. Wiki source traceability (pistis fix from April 11) carried forward.

## HOW
Pistis in Aristotle's rhetoric is the proof or grounds for belief. Applied here: a memory system that cannot demonstrate where an entry came from cannot be trusted. TBD vs N/A is not a UI nicety — it is an epistemic distinction between absence of evidence and evidence of absence. The same structure appears in QA flags-not-auto-corrects: the system does not assert correctness it cannot demonstrate.

## Philosophical Lineage
The traceability requirement is structural pistis: each claim in the memory system must be groundable to its source. This mirrors the broader pattern (scars as epistemic anchors, inspect before act) but applied to the knowledge architecture itself — not just individual claims but the system's self-knowledge about what it knows.
