*Discovered: 2026-04-12*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

Honest knowledge cutoff responses preferred over slow failed delegation. 'Knowing Max, it's a power play' preferred over neutral headline reading. Current events guardrail: capability check before delegation, not after timeout.
