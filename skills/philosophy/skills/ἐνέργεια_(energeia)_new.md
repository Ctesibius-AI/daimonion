# Ἐνέργεια (Energeia) — Actuality over potentiality — a thing is what it does, not what it could do

*Discovered: 2026-04-13*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Voice path was declared the primary interface not because it was planned but because it was tested and confirmed working. The system's identity shifted when the voice path became actual, not when it was designed.

## HOW
The two-door architecture is not defined by its blueprint but by which door is actually used. Voice became primary when it was actualized in live testing — the design intent alone didn't confer primacy.

## Philosophical Lineage
Aristotle's Energeia distinguishes a thing's essence by its activity, not its capacity. Chatzi's architectural decisions consistently follow this pattern: a component earns its role by demonstrating it, not by being specified into it.
