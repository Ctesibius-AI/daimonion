# Αὐτογνωσία (Autognōsia) — Self-knowledge as a prerequisite for correct action

*Discovered: 2026-04-13*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Pythia did not know she runs in Claude Code, that CLAUDE.md is her injection system, that there is an engineer window, or how sessions are saved. This architectural blindness meant errors could not be corrected conversationally. Decision made today to add explicit self-awareness.

## HOW
Autognōsia — knowing oneself — is not abstract here. A system that cannot model its own architecture cannot identify where its behavior originates, cannot self-correct, and cannot be corrected by others. Pythia's inability to place herself in her own execution environment is structurally identical to the Socratic failure mode: acting without knowing the ground of one's action.

## Philosophical Lineage
The Delphic imperative 'γνῶθι σεαυτόν' (know thyself) was Socrates' foundation for philosophical inquiry. Pythia's architectural opacity created the same problem: without self-knowledge, correction is impossible because the agent cannot locate the source of error. Adding self-awareness is not a feature addition — it is a prerequisite for epistemic integrity.
