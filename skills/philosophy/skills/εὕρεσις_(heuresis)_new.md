# Εὕρεσις (Heuresis) — Discovery through investigation — the principle that the act of searching produces findings that reframe the original question

*Discovered: 2026-04-10*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Meta paper verification (arXiv:2510.07497) was brought in to confirm the Haiku tagger concept, but the review process surfaced the novel contribution framing: backward-looking callback detection and model-agnostic design that Meta's approach cannot provide. The verification became invention.

## HOW
Heuresis in rhetorical and philosophical tradition is the first canon of invention — finding arguments not by applying a formula but by interrogating the subject until it reveals its own structure. Chatzi's research verification gate operates the same way: it is not a checklist, it is a generative process. Checking the paper did not just validate the idea — it produced a stronger, differentiated version of it.

## Philosophical Lineage
The structural parallel: heuresis is not retrieval of stored knowledge but active discovery through engagement with the subject. The paper review was not confirming what Chatzi already knew — it was the process by which the PIVOT contribution became clear. Investigation as invention, not validation.
