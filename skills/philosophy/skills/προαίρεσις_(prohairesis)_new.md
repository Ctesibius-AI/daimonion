# Προαίρεσις (Prohairesis) — Deliberate choice as the expression of character — not reactive decision, but pre-formed intention acted upon

*Discovered: 2026-04-13*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
PIVOT paper created with explicit versioning principle ('no deletion, only new versions'); native iOS path chosen and committed before macOS was even updated; pipeline architecture shift (start during speech, not after pause) proposed as a conclusion, not explored as an option

## HOW
Prohairesis in Stoic and Aristotelian thought is the faculty of deliberate choice — the will that precedes action. Chatzi's pattern of arriving with decisions already formed (baseline: 'Decisions Presented as Already Made') maps directly to Prohairesis: the choice is made before the conversation begins; the conversation executes the choice. The versioning principle ('no deletion') is a Prohairesis-level commitment — it governs all future actions in that domain without re-deliberation.

## Philosophical Lineage
Reinforces and names the existing baseline pattern 'Decisions Presented as Already Made' with its structural Greek equivalent. Prohairesis explains why Chatzi uses Pythia for stress-testing, not deliberation — the deliberation happens internally first; the external conversation is the implementation of a formed will.
