# Τέχνη (Technē) — Craft knowledge — knowing not just what to do but the ordering and sequencing that makes the work coherent.

*Discovered: 2026-04-13*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Cron job sequencing (RSS 2:55 → weight 3:00 → Night Processor 3:05), pipeline sequencing (classify → retrieve → generate), phase sequencing (Phase 7 deferred). User consistently treats ordering as substantive, not incidental.

## HOW
Technē in Aristotle is distinguished from mere habit by understanding the *why* of procedure — the craftsman knows the causal structure, not just the steps. User's sequencing decisions are always causally grounded (retrieval depends on classification; race conditions require temporal ordering). This is craft knowledge, not rule-following.

## Philosophical Lineage
Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics VI.4 — Technē as productive knowledge with rational principle. The pipeline architecture embodies this: each ordering decision is reasoned, not arbitrary.
