# Μαιευτική (Maieutics — Socratic midwifery) — The method of drawing out knowledge already present through questioning, rather than delivering conclusions from outside.

*Discovered: 2026-04-13*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
User explicitly named brainstorming sessions as the primary design mechanism: 'the April 6 session started as a latency bug fix and through questioning redesigned the entire architecture. Questions like why does the live log reset? and where does a report go? exposed design gaps before they became implementation debt.' User explicitly praised this dynamic as a valuable design tool.

## HOW
The questions did not add external knowledge — they revealed what the architecture implied but had not yet articulated. The design gaps were latent; the questions made them visible. This is structurally identical to maieutics: the knowledge was already in the system, the questioning brought it to birth.

## Philosophical Lineage
Socrates in the Theaetetus: the midwife does not give birth herself but assists the other in delivering what is already theirs. Pythia's brainstorming role is not to propose solutions but to ask questions that force the architecture to reveal its own contradictions. The principle operates the same way: no external injection of answers, only skilled questioning that surfaces what is already there.
