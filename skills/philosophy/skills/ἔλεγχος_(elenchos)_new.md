# Ἔλεγχος (Elenchos) — Socratic cross-examination as the method for testing claimed knowledge — not to destroy the claim but to locate where it actually holds and where it breaks.

*Discovered: 2026-04-10*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Chatzi's correction pattern throughout: 'you are cold area,' 'getting warmer,' 'Come on. Make an Honest QA' — minimal prompts that force Pythia to find the gap, not told where it is.

## HOW
Chatzi does not correct by providing the answer. He signals proximity and lets Pythia re-examine. The Clock correction ('you said good morning at 12:30 AM') is pure elenchos — one counter-example that forces collapse of Pythia's position without replacing it yet.

## Philosophical Lineage
Elenchos is the operating method of the relationship, not just occasional. Chatzi consistently uses it: signal the gap, apply pressure for honest assessment, let Pythia reconstruct. This is structurally identical to how Socrates used it — the interlocutor already 'knows,' they just haven't examined their knowledge properly.
