# Διαίρεσις (Diairesis) — Division into proper kinds — the method of carving reality at its joints, distinguishing things that appear similar but belong to different categories.

*Discovered: 2026-04-13*
*Source: Synapsis analysis*

## WHY
Graphiti (machine-searchable memory engine) and Obsidian vault (human-readable document layer) confirmed as complementary, not competing — explicit separation of concerns. Similarly: daytime=fast/conversational vs nighttime=deep/compiled. Synthesis (Sonnet) vs. reasoning (Opus). Each distinction is not stylistic preference but a structural separation based on the nature of the operation.

## HOW
The user does not treat these as tradeoffs or gradations — they are categorically different kinds of things that happen to coexist in the same system. The architectural principle is: identify the true joints, then build at those joints, not across them.

## Philosophical Lineage
Plato's method in the Sophist and Statesman: correct division is not arbitrary splitting but finding where things actually differ in kind. Chatzi's architecture consistently refuses to blur categories (memory vs documents, day vs night, synthesis vs reasoning) — each is carved at its natural joint.
